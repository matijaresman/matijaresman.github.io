Wriggle

Editorial Booklet


A booklet inspired by the song of the same name by clipping., which lyrically thematizes sex work.

The publication clashes two contrasting perspectives of the city of Hamburg: on the one side the Reeperbahn, the famous red-light disctrict and on the other one of the country’s biggest art museums, the Hamburger Kunsthalle. Images from the Reeperbahn which went through the process of sonification (editing the images with audio-editing software and whereby producing a glitchy effect) are collaged with edited images from the museum’s online collection, combined with experimentally typeset lyrics from the song it is based on.
Tools:
Audacity, Gimp, Adobe Photoshop
