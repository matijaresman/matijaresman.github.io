Spider Cider

3D Animated Music Video


A music video for the Oh Sees song of the same name, created using 3D scans of everyday household objects from the designer’s apartment — a coffee pot, toaster, washbasin, etc.
Tools:
Blender, Polycam, Adobe PremierePro
Each object is brought to life with a pair of googly eyes that dance along to the rhythm of the song, mixing humor with lo-fi digital surrealism:
