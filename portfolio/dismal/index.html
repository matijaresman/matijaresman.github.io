Dismal

Type Tool & Generative Typeface


A generative typeface and digital type tool based on the principles of diffusion-limited aggregation, a process whereby particles undergoing a random walk due to Brownian motion cluster together to form aggregates of such particles.

→ Link to Tool
Tools:
p5.js, JavaScript, Glyphs, HTML, CSS
